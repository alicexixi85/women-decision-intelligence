## 2. Count six constraints / 逐项核对六类现实约束
Give each row a concrete figure or write “Unknown”; don't mistake a promise for accessible resources.  
每一项尽量写出具体数字；不清楚就写“未知”，不要把口头承诺当成能实际支配的资源。

| Constraint / 约束 | Question / 需要回答的问题 | My reality / 我的真实情况 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cash / 现金 | What cash can I actually access after fixed costs and debt? 扣除固定开支与债务后，真正可支配的钱有多少？ | ____ |
| Time / 时间 | What hours and deadlines cannot be moved? 哪些时间和期限不能改变？ | ____ |
| Care / 照护 | Who depends on my unpaid work, and what would replacement cost? 谁依赖我的无偿照护？替代成本是多少？ | ____ |
| Safety / 安全 | Is any option unsafe, coercive, or dependent on secrecy? 是否存在人身安全、胁迫或隐瞒风险？ | ____ |
| Control / 实控权 | Who can actually approve, transfer, spend or revoke the resources? 谁真正有批准、转移、使用或撤回资源的权力？ | ____ |
| Institutions / 制度门槛 | Which contracts, benefits, permits, or rules need independent checking? 哪些合同、福利、许可或法规需要独立核实？ | ____ |

## 3. Separate facts from guesses / 将事实与猜测分开
For each important statement, mark **KNOWN**, **ASSUMED**, **UNKNOWN**, or **SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE**.  
对关键判断分别标注 **已知、假设、未知、合成示例**。例如：

- “I can live for 5 months on savings” → KNOWN only if the figures and access are checked.  
  “存款足以生活五个月”→ 只有核实金额、支出和可支配权后，才能列为已知。
- “My family will help if things go wrong” → ASSUMED until arrangements are confirmed.  
  “如果失败，家人会资助”→ 未得到具体可执行承诺之前，只能视为假设。

## 5. Safety first / 安全优先
If a choice involves threats, stalking, coercion, abuse, or immediate danger, prioritize safety and trusted local support instead of treating this worksheet as a substitute for professional help.  
如果涉及威胁、跟踪、胁迫、虐待或即时危险，应优先保障安全并寻求当地可靠支持，而非依赖本表替代专业服务。
