# Attribution & Source Boundaries

This repository organizes publicly documented information into a structured decision-intelligence knowledge base.

## What this license covers

The MIT license applies to original repository-authored material to the extent that it is copyrightable, including the framework structure, schemas, decision workflows, and original editorial organization.

It does not grant a blanket license to third-party material, trademarks, photographs, long quotations, or source documents referenced by this repository. Those remain subject to their respective rights and licenses.

## Source policy

Case files should distinguish documented fact, attributed statement, interpretation, and inference. Public facts are not presented as original research. Source attribution is maintained so readers can verify the underlying record.
